# Visual references and dimensional scope

Official McLaren Racing, “Secrets of an F1 garage” (25 November 2017):
https://www.mclaren.com/racing/latest-news/mclarenracing/article/secrets-of-f1-garage/

The official article's actual garage photograph was downloaded and visually inspected (research-only image, not included in the repository). Observed features that informed this asset: white garage wall systems, black lower storage, overhead technical gantry and coiled service lines, computer workstations along the side, rear equipment storage and working positions around both sides of the car. The new game's palette retains the supplied reference's red tool cabinets and warm cedar/glazed upper story, rather than copying McLaren branding. Reference photo is research-only, not embedded as a game texture.

Additional verified official garage-tour link, not a dimensional source:
https://www.mercedesamgf1.com/news/inside-an-f1-garage-or-f1-esports-garage-tour-at-spa

The 6m bay spacing, approximately 12m building depth and ~3.2m clear service height are game-design choices based on the provided car/crew geometry and maneuvering room. They are NOT represented as FIA requirements, measured team garage dimensions or certification of compliance.
